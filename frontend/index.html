OUR STORY

More than coffee. It's a feeling.

Otto Coffee started with a simple idea: create a place where great coffee and good conversations come together.

And then there was Otto.

Our black cat quickly became the unofficial owner of the coffee shop. He sleeps near the espresso machine, watches every customer, and somehow always knows when someone needs another cup.
